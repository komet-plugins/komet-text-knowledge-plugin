# komet-text-knowledge-plugin

Conceptification, ANFication and semantic indexing of stamped documents, as a Komet plugin:
finding the words that name concepts in the knowledge base, building ANF statements from them,
and indexing documents by what they state.

The design is the assembly *From Text to Compounding Knowledge* in `ike-lab-documents`
(module `text-to-knowledge`, starting from the topic `dev-concept-recognition`).

Built as a member of the `ike-komet-wsr` working set. Tracked in
[IKE-Network/ike-issues#1372](https://github.com/IKE-Network/ike-issues/issues/1372).
